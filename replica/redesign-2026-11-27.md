# Redesign, 27 Nov 2026: Pin16 laid out like Carista, in Pin16's own brand

Owner's request: "redesign the app making it better and closer to how carista is designed".

Sources: `replica/recording-review-cupra/summary.md` and `part1.md` to `part4.md` (Cupra, 15 Nov 2026), `replica/recording-review/review_part1.md` to `review_part4.md` (Audi A6, 11 Nov 2026), `replica/recon.md`. `replica/ux-review-2026-11-16/` holds screenshots only, no notes. Only what the notes record is stated here. Where the notes say "not seen", that is said.

Taken from Carista: layout, grouping, order and flow. Not taken: colours, icons, artwork, logo, exact wording. Pin16 keeps burnt orange on graphite, the port mark, Space Grotesk wordmark, `src/theme.ts` tokens and `src/ui.tsx` components.

## 1. How Carista structures its screens

### Home, once a car is connected

- Top to bottom: a promo card, then "VIN:" and "Voltage:" as two plain lines (voltage updates live), then 5 full-width menu cards, then a promo card, then an outlined "Disconnect" button at the bottom.
- The 5 cards, in this order, each with an icon, a title, a one-line subtitle and a chevron: Car health check (tag "NEW"), Customise, Diagnose, Service, Live data.
- The 3 tabs below are Home, Garage, More. Garage holds the cars and their history screens (Car diary, Fuel consumption, Customisations history, Diagnostics history, Service tools history). More holds help and app settings.
- One job per card, one tap to start it. No status clutter on Home; the car's identity is two lines.

### Customise

- Opens with a loading screen ("Checking your vehicle for available customisations", a percentage, 3 tips). 32 s on the Cupra, 84.5 s on the A6. After that every category opens at once (values are cached).
- Hub: a short description, a highlighted "Most Popular" card, a heading "All customisations", then one row per category with a chevron. 8 categories on the Cupra, 17 on the A6 (for example Chassis & Engine, Dings & Warnings, Instruments: Language & Units, Lights: Bulb Checks, Lights: Exterior, Locking: Automatic, Mirrors, Windows & Sunroof, Wipers & Washer).
- Category screen: a large title, then one card per setting: the setting's name in white and the car's current value below it in capitals (ENABLED, DISABLED, YES, NO, OFF, "20 SECONDS", "80 %", "GREAT BRITAIN", "TYPE 1", "19014 miles"). The same screen mixes on/off settings, numbers with units and named choices.
- Settings the car lacks are not listed (the Cupra got 36 settings, the A6 about 130). The notes record one exception: on the A6 convertible-roof rows were listed on a car that probably has none.
- Changing a value: tapping a card opens a screen titled with the setting, a list of radio options (the current one ticked, for example Disabled, US/Canada style, Euro style 1 to 3; or Type 1 to Type 10) and a full-width "Save" button that stays grey until a different option is picked. 2-state settings were not tapped in the recordings, so how they change is not known.
- Restore or backup: not seen in either recording. The Garage car page has a "Customisations history" row; on the A6 it was empty ("no history is available yet").
- A share icon sits top right of the hub. "Most Popular" was never opened.

### Diagnose run and results

- One screen titled "Diagnose" with a short description and a summary card of two rings: "ECUs scanned" (blue) and "Faults detected" (red). Both count up while the scan runs.
- The module list streams in place under the card: the module being read shows a spinner, a finished module shows its name and "FAULTS: n" (red text and number when n is above 0), an unreachable one shows "CANNOT CONNECT" (red) or "FAILED TO CHECK". 20 modules listed on the Cupra, 12 or more on the A6 (Basic OBD2, CAN network gateway, Engine, Transmission, ABS, Parking brake and so on). About 55 s for 11 modules on the A6.
- Fault rows sit inside the module's card: a red dot, the code in blue, a dash and the description, with an "AI insights" and a "More Data" button per fault (More Data is freeze frame; the AI button is not copied).
- One blue "Reset" button at the foot of each module card that has faults, and a final "Reset all" when the scan is done.
- A "Car health check" is a separate card on Home: a conditions dialog (stationary, engine idling, warm, AC off), then a result screen.

### Service

- Opens with a loading screen with a percentage (65.5 s on the A6 for 13 tools, about 5 s on the Cupra for 3; 6 tools across runs).
- A list of cards, each with an icon, a title and a chevron. A6: Used car checks, Service indicator reset, Electronic parking brake service, DPF regeneration, Fuel system prime, Throttle body adaptation, HVAC flap calibration, Instrument cluster colours test, Instrument cluster gong test, Climatronic display segment test, Detailed ECU info, Emission tests, Acceleration test.
- Every tool screen uses one template: title, a status chip ("Not started yet"), a plain description, a grey warning chip, a filled "Start" button (or OPEN and CLOSE, or "Reset" per indicator) and an outlined "Diagnose" button.

### Live data

- Opens with a loading screen with a percentage (24.5 s on the A6).
- A menu of categories with icons and chevrons: 11 on the A6 (Transmission live data, Airbag crashes count report, Service interval info, Car battery info, Engine turbo live data, HVAC live data, Engine data, Diesel Exhaust Fluid level, DPF info, Vehicle mileage information, Basic OBD2 live data), 3 on the Cupra (HVAC live data, Vehicle mileage information, Basic OBD2 live data).
- Each category opens with "Communicating with vehicle", then a list of parameter rows (name on the left, value and unit on the right, refreshing). An outlined "Diagnose" button sits at the foot.

## 2. Screen by screen: what Pin16 does today, what changes

### Car tab, connected (`src/app/(tabs)/(car)/index.tsx`)

Today: a small meta row (badge, "Connected", Disconnect), a grey line of year, fuel and platform, "Which car" notice, one big Health card (figure, 3 stat tiles, 2 buttons), then 4 half-width tiles (Customise, Live data, Service, History), Learn my car, Driving, This car and Expert groups. Customise shows "Change settings", not a count. The VIN sits in a group near the bottom.

Change:
- A car header card: make badge, car name, year, fuel, platform, VIN tail in Menlo, a "Connected" pill, Disconnect.
- 4 full-width job cards in Carista's order: Diagnose (the health check with the last result, engine light, self-tests, battery, See faults and Check again), Customise (count of settings for this car), Service (due state), Live data (sensor count). Each card is one tap.
- Everything else under one "More tools" heading with the existing groups: History, Learn my car, Driving, This car, Expert, Your cars. Each feature stays within 2 taps of the tab.

### Customise (`src/app/customise/index.tsx`)

Today: a hero card, category chips named by the data file (Instrument cluster, Lights, Locking and alarm and so on), a group per data category with a row per setting (name, description, switch), then Adjustable values as steppers, chips or a typed field inside rows, then Get more settings, Backups, Coding history, Change timeline, guides. No search. No current value shown in words on the row. Backups and Coding history sit far down the page.

Change:
- Top: search field, filter chips All, Changed from original, then the categories; rows for Put back original, Coding history and Change timeline, open from the top.
- One heading per category in a fixed order: Lights, Locking, Windows and mirrors, Instruments, Warnings and chimes, Comfort, Other. The mapping is in the new `src/coding/categories.ts` (item id, then title keywords, then the data file's own category). New items get a category without editing `items.ts`.
- Each setting is one row: the name, the car's current value on the right (On, Off, or the value with its unit). 2-state settings keep their switch in the row and write at once with no confirm. Multi-value settings (adjustable values) open a picker and write on pick. Only Put back and Forget ask first, as before.
- Settings the car does not have stay hidden. A setting with `unconfirmed: true` (or low confidence) carries a small "Unconfirmed" tag.
- Differs from Carista on purpose: no Save button after picking (owner's rule: writes at once), and one list per screen with headings and chips, not a hub of category rows (Pin16 reads on open and holds a short list).

### Diagnostics (`src/app/diagnostics.tsx`)

Today: a hero card with a module ring and a percentage while running; the list shows only modules that have answered, then on finish faulty modules each get their own group with always-open fault rows and a per-module clear row, healthy modules in "No faults", plus a "Clear all faults" row in a tools group among 4 other rows. The Check again button comes first.

Change:
- While running: one list of the car's modules. The module being read shows a spinner, a finished one ticks to "No faults" or "N faults", one that cannot be read says so. Modules still to ask show "Waiting" (when the car's modules are known), so the list shows progress like Carista's.
- When done: one list, modules with faults first. A module with faults opens to its faults in plain text with the code. Modules with no faults follow, then ones that could not be read.
- One clear button for everything ("Clear all faults", same confirmation and the same rules). The per-module clear stays inside the opened module.
- The saved result (`LastScan`) uses the same heading and row pattern.

### Service (`src/app/service.tsx`)

Today: intro text, a "Showing methods" notice, long step cards for each method, intervals, reminder, then "Reset with the adapter" at the end.

Change: the same job-card header as the Car tab (due state), a "Service tools" group of rows first (Reset with the adapter, plus the car-specific tools that already exist), then the reset methods, then the reminder groups. Same card and row patterns as the other 3 jobs.

### Live data (`src/app/live.tsx`)

Today: straight into tiles and long sections.

Change: the same job-card header (value counts for this car) above the existing live screen, so the 4 areas read as one app. The live engine itself is unchanged.

## 3. Out of scope or not known

- Carista's loading screens with percentages and tips: not copied; Pin16 opens on cached values and shows progress in place.
- Carista's "Most Popular", promo cards and share icons: not copied.
- Whether Carista offers a 2-state switch or a radio list for on/off settings: not seen.
- Carista's restore or backup: not seen. Pin16 keeps its own (Put back original, Coding history).
