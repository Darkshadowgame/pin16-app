# 2007 SEAT Leon Cupra: customisation settings, gap check of 27 Nov 2026

Scope: the owner's car (1P, PQ35, older modules over TP2.0 / KWP2000). Codings read on 27 Nov 2026 are quoted as given. Companion to `leon-labels-2026-11-22.md`, `leon-listen-2026-11-21.md`, `vag-tweaks.md` and `recording-review-cupra/summary.md`. Ross-Tech label (.lbl) files were not opened. Web pages were read through a fetch tool that summarises pages: where it said it had paraphrased, the quote is marked (paraphrase). Nothing was committed or pushed.

Grades: High = Ross-Tech wiki, a real write captured on this car, or 2 independent sources that agree for this module family. Medium = 1 detailed source for the exact family. Low = single vague post, sibling family, or a bit whose value on this car contradicts the source.

Grading note: for the 09 bits, the second source for several High grades is the other tool's own screen on this car (recording of 15 Nov 2026, `recording-review-cupra/summary.md`, "Customise" table). That is a read of the car, not a captured write, so it is weaker than a write capture. Where I used it, both the setting name and the on or off state agree with the bit. It is marked "Shown" below.

## 4. What changed in the app (all under `src/coding/`, plus tests)

Added, High (17): 09 bits for brake, rear fog, reversing, number plate bulb checks, bulb check master, DRL via fog lights, extra high beam, US-style side lights off, rear wiper with front wipers (continuous and interval); cluster brake pad and washer warnings (short coding); adaptation rows 09 channels 4, 7, 8 and cluster channels 3 and 4.

Added, Medium (11): 09 high beam bulb check; 46 comfort close by remote; 46 windows after a door opens; 46 eight lock and unlock confirmation bits (bytes 10 and 11). Medium items carry `confidence: 'medium'`, the marker the files already use; no new field was needed.

Re-graded (existing rows): 5 bulb check rows (3 to High, 2 to Medium); auto lock and auto unlock Low to Medium; titles of the front side and rear side rows corrected to "front parking" and "rear parking" (the label says parking lights).

Demoted to Low (existing rows, contradicted by the Golf 5 layout or by the car): four confirmation bits in 46 bytes 6 and 7 (titles end "(older layout)") and synchronised mirrors (46 byte 9 bit 6).

Held to a 30-byte coding: DRL via separate lamps (byte 15 bit 6), DRL as position lights (byte 24 bit 7), fog cornering (byte 14 bit 7). On the car's 23-byte coding these bytes are percentage values or past the end.

Logic fix: `mergeItems` now keeps short-coding items with different flags apart (it merged the seat belt, brake pad and washer items into one). The duplicate checks in `__tests__/coding.test.ts` and `__tests__/codingMerge.test.ts` include the flag.

New tests: `__tests__/leonMods.test.ts`.
